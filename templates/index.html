Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Modern hospital networks struggle with severe operational friction: emergency department overcrowding, prolonged inpatient boarding, erratic staffing allocation, and unexpected patient readmissions. Traditional legacy Electronic Health Records (EHRs) operate as passive digital filing cabinets rather than proactive decision-support systems.

The OLA Command Center Paradigm: OLA Health OS was engineered as a decentralized, holographic command center that "floats above" siloed department operations. By continuously ingesting data across IoT wearable patches, smart bed load sensors, triage vitals, and meteorological forecasts, OLA Health OS provides unified predictive visibility that eliminates bottlenecks before they materialize.
