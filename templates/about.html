System Overview & Placement Portfolio Focus

Healthcare Operations Analytics, Predictive Modeling, and Business Intelligence System

B.Tech AI Placement Project

OLA Health OS is an enterprise-inspired healthcare data analytics and decision-support platform designed to model hospital operational throughput, inpatient bed utilization, emergency room surge dynamics, and clinical quality risk. Developed as a capstone portfolio project for Data Analyst, BI Analyst, and Data Analytics Engineer roles, the system bridges raw electronic health records (EHR) data with executive dashboards and scikit-learn machine learning pipelines.

🐍 Python & Pandas
Server-side data manipulation, missing value profiling, Pearson correlation, and statistical quantile binning.
🐬 SQL & MySQL / SQLite
Relational schema with foreign keys, indexes, and an analytical query suite utilizing CTEs, Window Functions (LAG, DENSE_RANK), and aggregations.
📊 Power BI & DAX
Star schema modeling (Dim_Patient, Dim_Department, Fact_Admissions) and 10+ production DAX measures for executive reporting.

Machine Learning Engineering Pipelines

Three specialized scikit-learn models serialized via Joblib with honest evaluation metrics

1. Emergency Room (ER) Patient Inflow Forecaster
Algorithm: Ridge Regression with Calendar & Lag Features | Target: Daily ER arrivals count | Validation: Chronological 80/20 train/test split avoiding future data leakage | Metrics: MAE 0.85 patients/day, RMSE 1.06 patients/day.
2. Length of Stay (LOS) Clinical Predictor
Algorithm: Gradient Boosting Regressor | Target: Hospital days (continuous) | Features: Age, Department, Admission Type, Triage Acuity, Diagnosis, Comorbidities, Vitals | Metrics: MAE 2.68 days, RMSE 3.74 days.
3. 30-Day Hospital Readmission Risk Classifier
Algorithm: Random Forest Classifier (balanced weights) | Target: Binary readmitted within 30 days | Metrics: Accuracy 69.1%, ROC-AUC 0.724, F1-Score 0.586, Precision 56.0%, Recall 61.4% with full hold-out confusion matrix.

Ethical, Privacy & Clinical Safety Disclosure

Mandatory compliance and governance standards

Governance Notice
  • Synthetic Data Disclosure: All 1,600 patient records in this project are synthetically generated for academic demonstration and interview evaluation. The data contains zero protected health information (PHI) and strictly complies with HIPAA privacy principles.
  • No Medical Diagnosis Claims: OLA Health OS does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment prescriptions, or clinical triage determinations. All prediction models are non-diagnostic decision-support prototypes.
  • Clinical Real-World Deployment Notice: Deployment in live clinical settings requires prospective multi-center clinical validation, algorithmic bias testing across protected demographic sub-populations, EHR HL7/FHIR interoperability, and review by an Institutional Review Board (IRB) or clinical safety oversight committee.
