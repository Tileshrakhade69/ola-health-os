Hospital Readmission Reduction Program (HRRP) Overview

Under healthcare value-based care frameworks (such as CMS HRRP), uncoordinated readmissions within 30 days of discharge incur significant financial penalties and reflect potential care transition gaps. Patients with multiple chronic conditions (comorbidities) and high acuity admission presentations represent the primary clinical cohorts for post-discharge intervention.
